## C unification audit — 2026-11-03

The current foldable source of truth is the C matrix, not the archived A/B control arrangement:

- `standard56` is 56dp media, 56 × 56dp endpoint targets, 6 × 36dp visible handlers, 72dp strip and 6dp ordinary gaps. `compact48` is 48dp media/targets, 6 × 28dp handlers, 64dp strip and 6dp gaps.
- The shared queue math derives `N` and endpoint media gap `P` after profile selection and endpoint safety margins. The 428dp C lanes naturally resolve to five standard thumbnails with `P=6`; the count is not a product limit.
- C preserves preview bounds across poses, moves only controls/queue, keeps upright queue vertical and CW90 queue horizontal, and checks CW90 outer margins as an approximately 1:1:1 visual relationship. Numeric margins remain per-device outcomes.
- A/B files remain rendered and hashed as `archived` evidence. Generic layout/aspect/recording sheets are labeled `generic-reference`; only `currentFiles` are current design inputs. The role list and hashes are in `review/manifest.json`.
- Documentation was audited for A/B-pending, 400dp-switch, global-horizontal-queue and fixed-visible-count wording. Remaining historical mentions are intentionally inside archive/history sections; runtime acceptance language remains explicitly incomplete.

## Deferred implementation validation

These tasks require native behavior or hardware, not another speculative SVG rearrangement:

- Actual window/cutout/navigation/taskbar insets, small-window fallback and horizontal tabletop panes.
- Camera buffer/crop/rotation metadata, front mirroring and preview/output agreement across cameras and displays.
- Continuous recording on physical rotation; exactly-once finalization on fold/unfold.
- False-trigger rates and release headroom for deletion/queue gestures; cancellation, edge ownership, reorder auto-scroll and interrupted input.
- Held-device left/right/two-hand reach, including side-dock choice and switching hysteresis.
- TalkBack, Switch Access, localization, large text and accessible feedback timeouts.
- Additional SVG engines beyond the recorded Chrome contexts; do not describe the documented antialiasing exception as universal pixel identity.

Foldable static design selection is complete with C. Runtime approval is pending calibration of window sizes, WindowMetrics/insets/hinge behavior, native gesture arbitration and physical reach/device evidence. Left-hand preference presentation, sensor-specific mirroring behavior and final gesture tuning should follow the implementation/device evidence above. Focus locking, exposure adjustment, iOS and non-capture product screens remain outside this round's scope.
